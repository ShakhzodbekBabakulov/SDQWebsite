# SDQ Animated Website — Project Brief

## Project purpose

Create a distinctive corporate website for SDQ Management Advisory Group that presents management consulting, 1C implementation, integration, training, and ongoing support through an animated toy-train journey.

The experience should make complex technical services feel understandable, organized, and human.

## Company foundation

Based on the current SDQ website:

- SDQ is an expert consulting company implementing and supporting 1C products in Uzbekistan.
- The company has been an official 1C partner since 2023.
- Its experience began with a consulting management group established inside O‘zbekiston Temir Yo‘llari in 2017.
- SDQ has automated accounting, taxation, payroll, and personnel management across several railway organizations.
- The company is working toward a unified financial and accounting information system for O‘zbekiston Temir Yo‘llari.
- Current services include 1C implementation, support, integrations, staff training, accounting restoration, analytics, reporting, and 1C Cloud.
- The current website reports more than 100 projects and over 20 professional certificates.

## Central creative idea

**“We keep business moving.”**

The train represents a company’s operations. Every carriage performs a different job, but they are connected and move toward one destination.

The locomotive represents leadership and direction. The carriages represent SDQ’s people, partnerships, expertise, technology, and support.

The visual tone should feel:

- Warm and approachable
- Technically capable
- Premium and trustworthy
- Proudly connected to Uzbekistan
- Playful, but never childish

## Scroll behaviour

Each deliberate scroll advances the train by one carriage:

1. The train enters.
2. One carriage stops in focus.
3. Its objects animate gently.
4. The headline and explanation appear over the clean white space.
5. The train moves again, introducing the next capability.

The words should remain live website text rather than being baked into the video. This keeps them sharp, editable, searchable, and easy to translate.

## First concept decisions

The visual system should use:

- A profile-oriented composition with depth
- A clearly identifiable SDQ mascot
- A ceramic 1C symbol
- Partner logos represented as tactile plush or molded-plastic objects
- A miniature office staffed by diverse support specialists
- Technology and communications objects
- A white studio environment with soft shadows
- Clear negative space for website text

The mascot and partner objects are exploratory—not final brand assets. The actual 1C and partner logos should be inserted from official source artwork during production.

## Creative principle

The train is not merely decoration: its physical structure explains SDQ’s business model. Keeping the animation and website words separate allows the same train artwork to work in multiple languages and across printed materials.
